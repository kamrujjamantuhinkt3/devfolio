Most importantly

AI Mindset & Engineering

I treat AI as part of the toolchain, not a shortcut around it. Used well it closes the distance between an idea and a working thing; used carelessly it just produces confident nonsense faster. The difference is entirely in how you hold it — so this is how I hold it.

  • Brief it like a teammate

    I give a model the goal, the constraints and what “done” looks like — the same brief I would give a person. Vague prompts produce vague code, and that is on me, not the model.

  • Verify before trusting

    Anything it hands back gets read, run and tested before it goes near a branch. Confident output and correct output are not the same thing, and only one of them ships.

  • Know what not to delegate

    Architecture, data modelling and anything touching security stay with me. AI helps me move faster inside a decision; it does not get to make the decision.

  • Read until it makes sense

    When a model solves something I could not, I do not just paste it. I read it until I understand why it works, because next time I want to be the one who knew.
