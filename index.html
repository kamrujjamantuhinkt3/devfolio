About me

The person behind the code

I’m a full-stack developer from Savar, Dhaka, completing my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil International University.

Becoming a developer was never a fallback for me — it was the goal. That passion is what pulled me toward web development: it is the one place where an idea can go from a blank file to something a stranger can open and use on the same day.

So I build across the whole stack, the part people click and the part that keeps it running. My degree gave me the fundamentals — data structures, algorithms, how machines actually think. Everything after that came from building things that did not exist yet, breaking them, and sitting with the error until it made sense.
